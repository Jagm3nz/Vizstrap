## Bloxstrap

Vizstrap is written from scratch, but borrows data and assets from [Bloxstrap](https://github.com/bloxstraplabs/bloxstrap):
the Roblox package map, the historical Roblox icons, the mod presets (2006/2013 cursors, old character sounds, the old
avatar editor background), the "Fake Byfron" loading style images and the custom loading theme format
("BloxstrapCustomBootstrapper" XML), so Bloxstrap's themes work in Vizstrap. The Catmoji and Windows emoji fonts are
downloaded when picked from Bloxstrap's `rbxcustom-fontemojis` release.

## Depth Anything V2 Small

The shaders download the depth model **Depth Anything V2 Small** (ONNX export by onnx-community, half precision) on first
use; it isn't part of Vizstrap. It is licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2/blob/main/LICENSE) by its authors (Lihe Yang et al.).
